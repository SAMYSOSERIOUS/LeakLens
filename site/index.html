LeakLens · churn model monitor

How far can we trust the churn model this month?

A live-business simulation on the KKBox music subscription data. Each week the job feeds in one more month of history, scores every customer whose membership runs out next month, and checks how its older predictions turned out.

Demo mode: the numbers on this page come from the built-in made-up sample data, not from KKBox. Run the pipeline on the real files to replace them.
The audit

Three popular public churn models, re-tested honestly

Score = AUC: how well the model ranks leavers above stayers. 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect. "As published" builds features from the whole data file, including the weeks after the prediction date. "Honest" only uses what was known on that date and is tested on a later month.

Model health

Accuracy, month by month

Each point is a month of predictions, checked once the answers were in. Customers get 30 days to renew, so a month's answers arrive about two months later. When accuracy falls below the floor, the job retrains the model.

What the job did

    Drift warning

    Do this month's customers look like the ones the model learned from?

    Drift score (PSI) compares this month's customers with the training customers, input by input. Below 0.1 means no real change, 0.1 to 0.2 is worth watching, above 0.2 means customers look different. Drift often shows up before accuracy can be measured.
