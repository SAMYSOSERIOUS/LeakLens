§01 — MECHANISM

How the future gets into the features

A churn model predicts on a cut-off date whether a customer will renew within 30 days after their membership ends. If any feature is computed from records dated after that cut-off, the model is partly reading the answer. On KKBox this happens quietly, because the transaction log covers the whole period and most notebooks aggregate it in one pass.

L1 · READ AFTER THE FACT

A value overwritten by the outcome

The membership end date and the latest payment date are read from each customer's last transaction. If the customer renewed, they already point past the cut-off.

    §05 — DECISION

    Who is worth an offer, in euros

    Set what an offer costs and what a lost customer is worth. The cut-off that maximises savings is rarely the default 50%; it sits wherever the expected value of keeping someone exceeds the cost of asking. It is picked on one month and the money is counted on the next.
